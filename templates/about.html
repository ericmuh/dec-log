"Decision Log" App

A simple system where users log decisions they made and their outcomes later.

Example

  • Decision: "Invest in Bitcoin"
  • Reason: "Market trend looked strong"
  • Outcome later: "Lost money / gained money"
  • Lesson learned

Features

Create

  • Add decision
  • Reason
  • Confidence level (1-5)

Read

  • List all decisions
  • View decision detail

Update

  • Add outcome later
  • Update lesson learned

Delete

  • Remove bad decisions
